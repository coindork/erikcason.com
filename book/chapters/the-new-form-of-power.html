The New Form of Power Hidden in Bitcoin

The oath of machines and the sovereignty of code

Crypto assets create a new kind of agreement that departs from the problems of sovereignty by banishing violence in exchange for economic enforcement.

In order to understand how cryptosovereignty functions and creates value, we first must speak to the very political philosophy of crypto-assets to unveil the non-monetary value found concealed within them. Understanding this dynamic is one of the hidden keys to understanding the difference between Bitcoin and shitcoins, and why of all crypto-assets in our world of today, there can only be one truly sovereign crypto asset.

While the economic functions of all crypto-assets are the most apparent aspect of their operations, we must start from their philosophical underpinnings of them to expound any tenable economic theory. It is here as well that we also find the hidden schism that will lead our line of thinking through understanding Bitcoin correctly through the core political game theory hidden within all crypto-assets. To unveil this schism, we need to understand what, if any political objectives motivated Satoshi Nakamoto with the creation of Bitcoin:

"Yes, [we will not find a solution to political problems in cryptography.] but we can win a major battle in the arms race and gain a new territory of freedom for several years. Governments are good at cutting off the heads of centrally controlled networks like Napster, but pure P2P networks like Gnutella and Tor seem to be holding their own." --Satoshi Nakamoto

This quote indicates that Satoshi understood cryptography as munition of war that was specifically developed for communication under duress, which perfectly served his philosophical political purpose. This is just one example of many breadcrumbs that Satoshi left, which hints towards the secret art of what cryptography really is. This is the inductive sort of understanding that is demanded to be thought of for someone to understand cryptography not as a layperson, or a mathematician in the world, but as the Nobody who is beyond any laws and has been forsaken by it. This is just one of many hints at why cryptography is so essential to the Bitcoin timechain and how the Nakamoto protocol solves the byzantine general's problem; which is really a problem of civil war.

Cryptography accepts the a priori nature of the State of Exception that all governments rule from today in order to render their legal lawlessness potent. It is no wonder that within a year of Nietzsche publishing "God is dead" marking the end of philosophy, that we would get a new beginning that was not yet understood with Kerckhoffs's principal as published in La Cryptographie Militaire.

Crypto understands that God is Dead and that he died a long longer -- that only man may save us now from the omnipotence states have laid claim to. But who could rise to become so great in a time of such desperate need? How could it be possible for someone, anyone to go beyond God himself to create new rituals and tasks to redeem us from the Abyss that engulfs all? How could the Last God be summoned and when we would know him to come to pass?

Only No-One, a No-body, an Anon could initiate a task so great that it would take decades for the profoundness of the task to reverberate because of how far beyond it could really be. Satoshi in understanding the nature of the power this would unlock, made the decision that in order to create a new form of economic value that would be beyond any State control, that he would need to expose himself to the possibility of the full force of the violence of the State as all prior examples to create a non-state money have proven. The only being in our world of today that is strong enough to stand against such a force is the being who cannot be seen, touched, or known. Only the Anon, whose real face I shall never know, the No-one who must become the Someone who is capable of saving us from The Danger that threatens to capture all.
