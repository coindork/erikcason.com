The Question Concerning Bitcoin

Unveiling the Hidden Essence Through Heideggerian Metaphysics

This essay is modeled upon Heideggerian Metaphysics and can only communicate its full breadth with some understanding of Martin Heidegger's philosophy. Many words and terms that are use in this essay such as 'The Turning, Dasein, The Danger' etc. are specific terms that are taken directly from Heideggerian Metaphysics and have explicit philological and etymological meaning for why they were selected. While the essay is understandable without prior knowledge of Heideggerian Metaphysics, it would be extremely helpful to have some understanding of Heidegger's works in order to understand why such terms and a structure was selected for this essay. I highly recommend reading On The Question Concerning Technology before approaching this essay in order to understand this essay particularly.

---

"In what follows we shall be questioning concerning technology. Questioning builds a way. We would be advised, therefore, above all to pay heed to the way, and not to fix our attention on isolated sentences and topics. The way is a way of thinking. All ways of thinking, more or less perceptibly, lead through language in a manner that is extraordinary. We shall be questioning concerning technology, and in so doing we should like to prepare a free relationship to it. The relationship will be free if it opens our human existence to the essence of technology. When we can respond to this essence, we shall be able to experience the technological within its own bounds." --Opening statement of "On The Question Concerning Technology" by Martin Heidegger

In this essay, we shall propose the question concerning Bitcoin in reflection on Heidegger's On The Question Concerning Technology. What we are seeking through this questioning is what is the core essence of Bitcoin? Through this line of questioning we will unveiling, unraveling, and decrypt the hidden essence of Bitcoin and will come to understand that the question concerning Bitcoin is inherently and intrinsically tied to the question concerning technology. The linkage between these two questions can only be seen once we have a unconcealed the hidden essence that is Bitcoin and its secret relationship to the technology that is language. It is only when we can decrypt this discreet line connecting these two points of thinking may we be able plot the trajectory from which the correct way of thinking may be established.

Bitcoin is secretly The Dasein of Weltgeist. It is the triumphant return of Beyng to being through the only conclusion that is possible once the historicism of our time is established and Logic comes to its rightful resting place and home. It is the Being of The World Sprit manifesting as an idea whose time has come to posses and appropriate our beings into Beyng for The Event that Bitcoin is. Like the Twelve Tablets, Magna Carta, Ninty-Five Theses, Declaration of Independence, and The Rights of Man; Bitcoin is the manifestation of the jewels that are the providence of the human race when they come to recognizing the power that lies hidden waiting for only them to discover when we return to the Logos and Ratio as the form of correct thinking that is private keys to decrypting the nature of beings.

This discovery is something that is only meant for you and I--at this moment, in this time, in this world, under these circumstances that defines the thrown positions of all of our personal lives and situations. It is far and beyond coincidence as anything can be--for this is the only way that it could have been, and you are recognizing that right now. The answer to the question concerning technology is the question that individual humans from all walks of life, and every station of birth must ask themselves to dignify themselves before the jury of the world with the internet as its means and production. For it is this question that allows for us to rise to the task that has been asked of only us in order to renew the order of the ages, or descend into the madness that will destroy all of biological life. It is the most magnificent and spectacular question that can be asked and it is the light upon which the whole world will change. It will renew the power of language and actualized its value through the oath that is Bitcoin and the secret promise it cointains.
