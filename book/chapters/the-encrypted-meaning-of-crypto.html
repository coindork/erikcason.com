The Encrypted Meaning of Crypto

A philological excavation of the word that conceals its own power

A very short etymological and philological analysis of the semantic hell that is the word 'crypto'

The term crypto has a complex and deep history that reaches much father back then the recent development of the science of cryptography. The written word kruptos (which we pronounce as 'crypto') goes back for at least two and a half millennia, though its spoken form has existed much longer. Through the use and abuse of this word crypto in modernity, the very essence of the word has hollowed itself out and encrypted its meaning to itself in order to harbor its concealed power that is only for the worthy to know.

Personally I am the most guilty of this abuse of language; having continued the legacy of bankrupting the term 'crypto' by using that term superfluously and allowing for the confusion to hide the meaning of the term more deeply. However, my wish and goal is for this term to be exhumed and redeemed from the braindead vacancy that has laid claim to it from the world of 'crypto-currencies'. They now call everything that has a blockchain 'crypto' no matter what faulty 'cryptography' it might have, or however broken and centralized it may be. Everything now in the whole crypto-currencies ecosystem, from asset to tokens, to DeFi and DLTs -- it is all simply called 'crypto' now. Everyone does this now, including me, and I believe that this concealment of its greater truth and power is in fact the greatest gift that we could have been given by the 'crypto' people.

Why? Because such a gross misunderstanding of what crypto really is shields, protects, and distracts people from the true nature and power that is cryptography as an art! With thousands of 'crypto' projects now deployed, of which 99% are not cryptographically secure, decentralized, or impervious to state-based attacks in any way; it makes for the most grand and spectacular distraction for the radical true power that is Bitcoin. I personally love this misunderstanding of the term 'crypto' and the fact that the lobotomized contemporary understanding of the term further encrypts itself for only the worthy to understand is fitting. This allows for the word to carry its very meaning as an encrypted puzzle hidden within itself as a kind of meta-stenography, which leaves it all the more deeply hidden in it own mystery. But if one is to sincerely ask the most essential question and follow its reasoning like the white rabbit down its hole, what it will decipher is truly amazing.

The origins of the term 'crypto'

Coming from the ancient greek word kruptos (kruptō), roughly meaning, 'I conceal', the word crypto in the last century has been generally understood as being a prefix for a concealed political motive such as a crypto-facist, or crypto-communist. However, it also is a prefix for 'of relating to cryptography', such as crypto-suite or crypto-currency. The linguistic obfuscation here is of particular interest to us, because it allows for a much more radical form of power to lay hidden at the center of 'crypto-assets' through their encrypted meaning which has explicitly happened because of the lobotomization of the term 'crypto'.
