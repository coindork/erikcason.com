The Encrypted Political Philosophy of Bitcoin

A brutal, lucid argument that sovereignty is not granted by institutions. It is engineered through cryptography, property, and voluntary coordination.

what the book argues

A philosophy of Bitcoin as political order.

CryptoSovereignty reads Bitcoin as an emergent political order grounded in property, cryptography, and non-violent coordination. It is not trading advice, not blockchain hype, and not institutional apologetics. It is for builders, thinkers, and readers who want the deepest why behind Bitcoin.
